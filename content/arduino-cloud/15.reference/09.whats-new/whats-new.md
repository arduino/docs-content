This page lists notable recent additions to Arduino Cloud and its documentation, grouped by area, with links to the pages that describe them.

## Get Started and Documentation

- **New documentation structure**: the Arduino Cloud documentation is organized by task, from [Get Started](/arduino-cloud/get-started/what-is-arduino-cloud) to [Reference](/arduino-cloud/reference/cloud-variable-types).
- **[Quickstart: Your First IoT Project](/arduino-cloud/get-started/quickstart-first-project)**: a single walkthrough from creating an account to controlling a board from a dashboard and your phone.

## Devices

- **[Add an UNO Q](/arduino-cloud/devices/add-uno-q)**: connect an UNO Q or VENTUNO Q to Arduino Cloud directly from Arduino App Lab. The board is provisioned automatically.
- **[Lock a Device to Your Account](/arduino-cloud/devices/device-lock)**: keep a board tied to your account, or unlock it so someone else can set it up.
- **[Bluetooth® provisioning](/arduino-cloud/devices/network-configuration#bluetooth-provisioning)**: GIGA R1 WiFi joins the boards that can be set up over Bluetooth® LE, and `arduino-cloud-cli device provisioning migrate` prepares a Provisioning 1.0 board for it.

## Things & Data

- **[Smart Folders](/arduino-cloud/things-data/smart-folders)**: group Things, devices and dashboards with rules that keep each folder up to date automatically.

## Dashboards

- **[Image Map Widget](/arduino-cloud/dashboards/image-map-widget)**: place live Cloud Variables, controls and labels on a custom image, such as a floor plan.
- **[Colored Light and Color variables](/arduino-cloud/reference/cloud-variable-types#color-and-colored-light)**: work with RGB values through the `Color` class.

## Templates

- **[Templates with Triggers](/arduino-cloud/get-started/start-from-a-template)**: templates can include Triggers in addition to Things and Dashboards, so the automations are created together with the project.

## IoT Remote App

- **[Use Your Phone With Arduino App Lab](/arduino-cloud/iot-remote-app/arduino-app-lab)**: stream your phone's sensors and camera to an UNO Q or VENTUNO Q running Arduino App Lab.

## Spaces & Plans

- **[Spaces Overview](/arduino-cloud/spaces-plans-account/spaces-overview)**: see statistics, plan usage and devices across every space you belong to.
- **[Space Statistics](/arduino-cloud/spaces-plans-account/space-statistics)**: monitor Things, devices, members, ingested records and Trigger activations for a space.
- **[Members, Roles & Permissions](/arduino-cloud/spaces-plans-account/members-roles)**: see which actions each Business and Education role can perform.
- **[Custom Branding](/arduino-cloud/spaces-plans-account/custom-branding)**: white-label a shared space with your own logo, colors, custom URL and sign-in options.

Arduino Cloud is available on a free tier and several paid tiers. Every tier
includes the core workflow — devices, Things, variables, dashboards, and the
editor — but the resource limits and access to advanced features differ. This
page summarizes what changes between tiers; for the authoritative,
feature-by-feature breakdown see the
[Plans & Feature Matrix](/arduino-cloud/reference/plans-feature-matrix).

## Plan Tiers

| Tier | For |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Trying the platform and small personal projects |
| **Maker** | Hobbyists wanting more Things, retention, and advanced features |
| **Paid / Business** | Teams and companies needing fleet management, RBAC, and longer retention |

## What Differs Between Tiers

The limits below are the ones most likely to influence which plan you need:

| Limit / Feature | Free | Maker | Paid / Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| Number of Things | Lowest | Higher | Highest / tailored |
| Data retention | Shortest | Longer | Longest (e.g. 1 year) |
| Over-the-air (OTA) updates | Limited | Included | Included |
| Triggers & alerts | Not included | Included | Included |
| Advanced dashboard widgets | Limited | Included | Included |
| Compile time per day | Lowest | Higher | Highest |
| Shared spaces & RBAC | Not included | Not included | Included |

## Choosing a Plan

Start on **Free** to validate a project end to end — it's enough to build a
complete IoT project. Move to **Maker** when you hit the Things limit, need
longer data retention, or want
[Triggers](/arduino-cloud/automations/triggers-and-alerts) and advanced
widgets. Choose a **paid/Business** plan when you need
[shared spaces](/arduino-cloud/spaces-plans-account/spaces),
[roles](/arduino-cloud/spaces-plans-account/members-roles), fleet management,
or extended retention.

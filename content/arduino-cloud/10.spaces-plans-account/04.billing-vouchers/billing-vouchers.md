Paid Arduino Cloud plans are managed from your account's plan and billing
settings, where you can enter payment information, redeem a voucher, and upgrade,
downgrade, or cancel a subscription. This page outlines each task; because the
billing interface changes periodically, exact menu paths are flagged for
verification.

## Where Billing Lives

Plan and billing settings are reached from your account menu — typically under a
**Plans** or **My Plans / Billing** area, where your current plan, usage, and
payment method are shown.

## Changing a Plan

From the plan settings you can:

- **Upgrade** to a higher tier for more Things, retention, or features — changes
  generally take effect immediately.
- **Downgrade** to a lower tier — be sure your usage fits the lower tier's
  [limits](/arduino-cloud/spaces-plans-account/plans-limits-quotas) first, as
  exceeding them can disable resources.
- **Cancel** a subscription — access to paid features ends at the close of the
  current billing period.

<Alert type="warning">

Needs verification: the precise behavior on downgrade/cancellation (grace

</Alert>

## Redeeming a Voucher

If you have a voucher or promotional code (for example from a kit, course, or
campaign), redeem it from the plans/billing area:

1. Open your plan or billing settings.
2. Find the **Redeem voucher** (or promo code) field.
3. Enter the code and confirm.

The associated plan or credit is then applied to your account.

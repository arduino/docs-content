## Overview

When a product reaches its End of Life, Arduino announces it in a bulletin, together with the date after which the product can no longer be bought.

## Lifecycle Stages

- **Active:** the product is on sale and recommended for new designs.
- **Not recommended for new designs:** the product is still on sale, but a newer product is a better choice.
- **Declared End of Life:** the End of Life has been announced and the End of Sales date is set.
- **Discontinued:** the End of Sales date has passed and the product is no longer available.

## Suggested Alternatives

When a newer product replaces one that reaches its End of Life, the [Product Index](/product-index/) lists it as the suggested alternative.

## Overview

Arduino supports each product with software updates and technical support from its release until its End of Support date, which is listed in the [Product Index](/product-index/).

## What Support Includes

- Updates to the cores, libraries and tools that the product relies on.
- Answers from the support team about the product and its documentation.
- Fixes for documentation errors.

## When Support Ends

After the End of Support date the product documentation stays online, but it is no longer updated.

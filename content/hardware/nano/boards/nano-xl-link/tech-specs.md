Here you will find the technical specifications for the Arduino® Nano XL Link.

**Note on operating voltage:** the Nano XL Link operates at +3.3 VDC. All digital and analog pins, including the I²C, SPI and UART interfaces, use +3.3 VDC logic levels. Applying +5 VDC signals to the board's pins without level shifting may damage the board.

**Note on Ethernet:** the Ethernet signals on the headers are the PHY's differential pairs and must be connected to an RJ45 connector with integrated magnetics, such as the one on the Nano Connector Carrier. Do not connect them directly to an Ethernet cable.

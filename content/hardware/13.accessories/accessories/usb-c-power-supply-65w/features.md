<FeatureDescription>

The Arduino® USB-C Power Supply (65W) delivers up to 65 W to USB-C devices through the USB Power Delivery (PD) protocol, across five voltage profiles (5, 9, 12, 15, and 20 VDC), automatically negotiating the optimal output for the connected device. It is the recommended power source for the Arduino® VENTUNO™ Q board, and features a foldable plug with interchangeable EU and UK options.

</FeatureDescription>

<FeatureList>

  <Feature title="Power Output" image="power">
    Five voltage profiles: 5, 9, 12, 15, and 20 VDC, with a maximum output of 65 W.
  </Feature>

  <Feature title="USB Power Delivery 3.0" image="usb">
    USB PD 3.0 support for automatic voltage and current negotiation.
  </Feature>

  <Feature title="Regional Plugs" image="connection">

  <Feature title="Safety Protection" image="security">

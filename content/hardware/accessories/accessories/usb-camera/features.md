<FeatureDescription>

The Arduino® USB Camera is a compact camera accessory for the Arduino® UNO Q and VENTUNO Q boards. Built around the Sony IMX376 image sensor, it captures up to 3840 × 2160 (UHD 4K) video and connects over USB as a standard USB Video Class (UVC) device, requiring no drivers. It combines an autofocus lens, an integrated LED ring light, and a built-in microphone for video capture, streaming, and vision-based applications.

</FeatureDescription>

<FeatureList>

  <Feature title="4K Video Capture" image="camera">
    Sony IMX376 image sensor capturing up to 3840 × 2160 (UHD 4K) at 30 fps in MJPEG.
  </Feature>

  <Feature title="Plug-and-Play UVC" image="usb">
    USB Video Class (UVC) compliance for automatic, driver-free recognition on the UNO Q and VENTUNO Q.
  </Feature>

  <Feature title="Autofocus Lens" image="focus">
    Autofocus from 20 cm to infinity, with an F/2.0 lens and an 80° diagonal field of view.
  </Feature>

  <Feature title="Ring Light & Microphone" image="light">
    Integrated LED ring light with warm and cool white settings, plus a built-in microphone for capture, conferencing, and content creation.
  </Feature>

</FeatureList>

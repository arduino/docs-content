The Arduino® USB Camera is a compact, plug-and-play camera accessory for the Arduino® UNO Q and VENTUNO Q. Built around the Sony IMX376 image sensor, it captures up to 3840 × 2160 (UHD 4K) video and is recognized automatically as a standard USB Video Class (UVC) device, with no drivers to install. It features an autofocus lens, an integrated LED ring light, and a built-in microphone.
